Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The WTF(x)? event is organised by Lucas Da Silva Barrão. For any question about your data, write to customerservice@wtfx.lu.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • The names and email addresses of the companions you register
  • Your ticket type, and on the night, whether you've checked in and how you paid

If you register companions, please let them know you're sharing their details with us.

Why we use it

Only to manage registrations and entry to the event: confirming your spot by email, checking you in at the door and contacting you if something about the event changes. The legal basis is the consent you give when you register. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Registrations are stored in Google Sheets and confirmation emails are sent through Google and Zoho Mail. These providers process the data on our behalf. Only the organising team and door staff can see the guest list.

How long we keep it

We delete all guest data within 30 days after the event.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time by writing to customerservice@wtfx.lu. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the Luxembourg data protection authority, the CNPD (cnpd.public.lu).
